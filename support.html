Memories Support

Need help with Memories? You can find answers to common questions below or contact us directly.

Login & Account

If you are unable to sign in, first check your internet connection and make sure you are using the correct account credentials.

If the problem continues, contact support and include as much information about the issue as possible.

Privacy & Blocking

Privacy settings can be managed from Settings > Privacy inside Memories.

Blocked accounts can be reviewed from Settings > Blocked Accounts.

Memory Invitations

Memory invitations appear in your notifications. You can accept or decline an invitation before becoming a member of the shared Memory.

Shared Memories

Shared Memories allow you to keep moments with other people in one place. Depending on the Memory and its settings, members can view shared photos and videos and save supported media to their own photo library.

Notifications

Notification categories can be managed from Settings > Notifications inside Memories.

Notification permission must also be enabled for Memories in the iOS Settings app.

Account Deletion

You can initiate deletion of your account directly from the account settings inside Memories.
