Privacy Policy

Who We Are

This Privacy Policy explains which personal data Memories processes, why, and what rights you have. Effective date: November 19, 2026.

Memories is operated by Samet Guerkan, who is responsible for the processing of your data (controller). You can reach us at supportmemories@gmail.com. Our postal address is listed in the Legal Notice.

Information We Process

Depending on how you use Memories, we process:

  • Account data: email address, username, profile name, biography and profile photo.
  • Content you create: albums, photos, videos, captions, stories, comments, likes and reactions.
  • Messages: text, photos, videos, voice messages, view once moments, Live Moment answers, message reactions and read status.
  • Social data: followers, follow requests, album memberships, invitations and blocked accounts.
  • Activity data: your online status while the app is open, the days on which you post (for your streak and ropes), your time zone, and one daily mark that you used the app (date only, for user statistics).
  • Photo details: the capture date and an approximate location (rounded to about 10 km) of photos and videos you add. We use this for trips, postcards and monthly recaps. If you set a home place, only you can see it.
  • Settings: privacy, notification and album settings, and the push token of your device.
  • Reports and support: problem reports, content reports and messages you send to support.
  • Technical data needed to run and secure the service, such as app version, device type and system version.

Camera, Microphone and Photos

Memories only accesses your camera, microphone and photo library after you allow it in iOS. We only receive the photos and videos you choose or capture. You can change these permissions at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Why We Use Your Data

  • To provide Memories: accounts, profiles, albums, stories, chats, Live Moments, streaks, ropes, trips and notifications (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, performance of our contract with you).
  • To keep Memories safe: preventing abuse, handling reports, enforcing our Terms and fixing errors (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, legitimate interest in a secure and working service).
  • To understand how many people use Memories each day, without tracking what they do (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Push notifications and device access, only if you allow them (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, consent). You can withdraw your consent at any time.

We do not show ads, we do not sell your data and we do not use third party tracking.

Who Can See What

  • Your profile, username and profile photo are visible to other users. With a private account, only approved followers see your albums, stories and followers.
  • Albums are visible according to their visibility. Private albums are only visible to accepted members.
  • Stories are shown for 48 hours. View once moments are shown to the recipient once.
  • People you chat with can see when you are online.
  • Your streak is shown on your profile from 3 days, and ropes are shown in chats, unless you turn off "Show My Streak".
  • Trip postcards and routes follow your trip setting. Your home place is only visible to you.

Other users can always take screenshots. Only share what you are comfortable sharing.

Service Providers

We use carefully selected providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase Inc. for hosting, database, sign in, file storage and realtime features.
  • Apple Inc. for delivering push notifications and iOS features.

Where data is processed outside the EU or EEA, for example in the USA, this is based on an adequacy decision of the European Commission (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework) or on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a push token for your device so we can deliver them. You can choose which notifications you get in Settings > Notifications, and turn them off completely there or in the iOS Settings app. Reminders such as the streak reminder are planned on your device.

How Long We Keep Data

We keep your data as long as your account exists or as long as it is needed for the purpose it was collected for.

Messages you delete for everyone are removed for all participants. Content you delete is removed from the service. Copies may remain in backups for a limited time before they are overwritten.

Data we must keep by law, or need to defend legal claims, is kept only as long as required.

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account in Settings > Delete Account. This deletes your account and your personal data, except data we are required to keep by law.

Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time.

To use your rights, contact supportmemories@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the authority where you live, or the authority responsible for us: Unabhängiges Landeszentrum für Datenschutz Schleswig-Holstein (ULD), Kiel, Germany.

Security

We use technical and organizational measures to protect your data, such as encrypted connections and access rules in our database. No online service can guarantee complete security.

Children

Memories is only for people who are at least 16 years old. We do not knowingly process data of younger children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Memories or the law changes. The current version is always available in the app and on our website.
