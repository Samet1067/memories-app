Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Memories collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the app.

The service is operated by Samet Guerkan.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Memories, we may process:

  • Account information, including your account identifier, username, profile name, biography, and profile image.
  • Authentication information necessary to create and maintain your account and session.
  • Social information, including followers, following relationships, follow requests, blocked accounts, and related relationship data.
  • User-generated content, including Memories, photos, videos, captions, comments, reactions, likes, and other content you choose to upload or create.
  • Shared-Memory information, including memberships, invitations, profile-visibility preferences, and activity associated with shared Memories.
  • Messages and story replies that you choose to send through the service.
  • Notification information, including notification preferences and device push-notification tokens.
  • Support information that you submit when reporting a problem or contacting support.
  • Technical information reasonably necessary to operate and secure the service, such as app version, device type, operating-system version, and service-related diagnostic information.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and maintain user accounts.
  • Provide profiles, Memories, shared Memories, messaging, notifications, reactions, comments, likes, invitations, and other app functionality.
  • Display content to people with whom you choose to share it.
  • Apply privacy, blocking, and profile-visibility preferences.
  • Deliver push notifications where you have enabled them.
  • Respond to support requests and investigate technical problems, abuse, or misuse.
  • Maintain security, prevent unauthorized access, and protect the integrity of the service.
  • Improve reliability and troubleshoot app functionality.

Photos and Videos

Memories allows you to select or capture photos and videos for Memories, shared Memories, and Private Memories.

Media that you choose to upload is stored so that it can be shown to users who have permission to access the relevant Memory.

Access to Private Memory media is limited to accepted members of that Private Memory.

You should only upload content that you have the right to use and share.

Messages and Interactions

Messages, story replies, comments, likes, and reactions may be processed and stored in order to deliver those features to their intended recipients and participants.

Blocking features may restrict communication and visibility between accounts.

Service Providers

Memories uses service providers to operate its technical infrastructure.

Supabase is used for backend services such as authentication, database functionality, realtime functionality, and storage.

Apple services may be used for platform functionality, including push notifications and features provided by iOS.

Information is shared with service providers only as necessary to provide, secure, maintain, or support the service, subject to their applicable terms and data-protection obligations.

Push Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app may register a device push token and associate it with your account so that relevant notifications can be delivered to your device.

You can disable notification permission in iOS Settings and manage supported notification categories inside Memories.

Content Visibility

Some information is visible to other users depending on the app feature and your settings.

Profile information may be visible to other users according to the app's privacy rules.

Public shared Memories may be visible to members of those Memories and may appear on profiles depending on membership and visibility settings.

Private Memories are restricted to accepted members of the relevant Private Memory. They are not intended to appear on public user profiles or in the public-facing feed.

Memories provides privacy settings, blocking controls, membership controls, and shared-Memory visibility controls to help you manage access to your content.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with applicable legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Content may remain stored while your account or the relevant Memory exists.

When information is deleted, copies may remain temporarily in backups or technical systems where immediate removal is not reasonably possible, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Account and Data Deletion

You can initiate deletion of your account from the account settings inside Memories.

Account deletion is intended to remove your account and associated personal data that we are not legally required to retain.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for security purposes, or for the establishment, exercise, or defense of legal claims.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal data, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to certain processing, or request a portable copy of certain information.

To make a privacy request, contact sametguerkan@outlook.de .

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

No online service or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Memories is not intended for children who are below the minimum age permitted to independently use an online service in their jurisdiction.

Users must meet any applicable minimum-age requirements and must not provide personal information in violation of applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service, our practices, or applicable requirements change.

The current version and effective date will be made available through the app and this website.
